# References papers

I believe it is of interest to read in detail the three related papers on Solar wind speed prediction at L1 from  full disc EUV images ([Upendran et al 2020](https://doi.org/10.1029/2020SW002478) ,  [Raju et al 2021](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11207-021-01874-6) , [Brown et al 2021](https://doi.org/10.1029/2021SW002976) ) 

As discussed, it would be interested to build a comparative table with the

- dataset used (which year, wavelengths)
- strategy for splitting into training/validation/test
- network architecture
- target/objective

For example, Raju et al use backcast instead of forecast: the measured solar wind (sw) speed is ballistically traced back to obtain the AIA image corresponding to the source region of the measure sw. (fast solar wind takes 3 to 4 days to reach 1AU, whereas slow solar wind may take up to 5 days.)
(This is also one of the reasons why in Bernoux et al they consider a time series of images, and not only one image.)

## ML-ready SDO dataset

There exist two versions

1. SDOMLv1 is described in [Galvez et al 2019](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.3847/1538-4365/ab1005) (imsize 1024x1024, time range [2010-2018], format is .npz) The links to the datasets are in the Appendix of Galvez et al paper.
2. In SDOMLv2, they extended the dataset further in time, added FITS keywords (see https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FITS for explanation of the FITS format), and consider a 512x512 imsize. The v2 is availble (in .zarr format) from AWS as a S3 bucket, see https://registry.opendata.aws/sdoml-fdl/ This website also contains github links to reproduce SDOMLv1 and SDOMLv2 datasets from original SDO data.
